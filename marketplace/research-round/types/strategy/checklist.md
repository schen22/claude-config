# Strategy checklist (in addition to the core checklist)

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| S1 | Does the TL;DR say the **mode** (external market or internal platform)? Is **market size** (external) or **demand** (internal: users, usage growth, cost trajectory) shown with its method, every input sourced? |
| S2 | Is every market, benchmark and competitor source **dated**, with anything older than 12 months flagged? |
| S3 | Are the **alternatives** covered: competitors and substitutes (external), or benchmarks and buy / build / extend options (internal), including "do nothing"? |
| S4 | Is **phasing by quarter**, with what we **won't do** this year and why? |
| S5 | Does every **KPI** have a definition (numerator ÷ denominator), baseline, 12-month target and data source, and is there a north star with guardrails? |
| S6 | Are **risks** listed with a leading indicator and what we'd do if it fires? |
| S7 | Does it say **what would change the plan**: the assumptions to re-check each quarter? |
| S8 | Internal platform only: is the **boundary** explicit (what the platform owns, what each product owns, the contract between them)? |
