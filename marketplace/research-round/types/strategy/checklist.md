# Strategy checklist (in addition to the core checklist)

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| S1 | Is the **market size** shown with its method (top-down and bottom-up), every input sourced, and the two compared? |
| S2 | Is every market and competitor source **dated**, with anything older than 12 months flagged? |
| S3 | Are the **main competitors and substitutes** covered (incl. "do nothing" / spreadsheets), not just the obvious ones? |
| S4 | Is **phasing by quarter**, with what we **won't do** this year? |
| S5 | Does every **KPI** have a definition (numerator ÷ denominator), baseline, 12-month target and data source? |
| S6 | Are **risks** listed with a leading indicator and what we'd do if it fires? |
| S7 | Does it say **what would change the plan** (the assumptions to re-check each quarter)? |
