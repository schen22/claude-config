# Round type: strategy

A question about **where to play and how to win over the next 12 months**: market, competitors,
customers, and what it takes to deliver. The output feeds a 12-month strategy document.
Phasing in Recommendation: **by quarter (Q1–Q4)**, plus **what we won't do** this year.
Also answer this type's checklist: [checklist.md](checklist.md).

## Lens per role (this type)

| Role | Covers |
|---|---|
| competitive-analyst | Competitors and substitutes: profiles, features, pricing, positioning, momentum (funding, launches, hiring), likely next moves; where the gaps are and how to win them |
| data-scientist | Market size, top-down **and** bottom-up with every input sourced; growth trends; the KPIs that track the strategy, each with baseline, target and data source |
| staff-software-engineer | Technical feasibility of each strategic option; architecture; security and privacy; build vs buy vs extend; technical and delivery risks; platform shifts that open or close options |
| ux-researcher | Customer segments, jobs to be done, pain points and willingness to pay, from evidence (reviews, studies, interviews); how to validate cheaply in Q1 |

Other roles keep their own lens and apply it at a 12-month horizon.

Default roles: competitive-analyst, data-scientist, staff-software-engineer, ux-researcher (up to 3 per round
unless all four are named).
