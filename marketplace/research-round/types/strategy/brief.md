# Round type: strategy

A question about **where to play and how to win over the next 12 months**. The output feeds a
12-month strategy document. Phasing in Recommendation: **by quarter (Q1–Q4)**, plus **what we
won't do** this year. Also answer this type's checklist: [checklist.md](checklist.md).

## Mode — say which in your TL;DR

- **External market:** a product sold to customers. Size the market; compare competitors.
- **Internal platform:** a shared platform used by internal teams (e.g. a developer platform).
  Size **demand** instead of a market (users, usage growth, cost trajectory); compare
  **external benchmarks and buy / build / extend alternatives** instead of competitors; and
  cover the **platform boundary** (what the platform owns vs each product team).

## Lens per role (adds to the agent's own)

| Role | In a strategy round, also covers |
|---|---|
| competitive-analyst | Market or benchmark landscape; momentum and likely next moves; where the gaps are |
| data-scientist | Market or demand size (top-down and bottom-up, every input sourced); north star and input metrics with baselines and 12-month targets; the experiment loop that adjusts the roadmap |
| staff-software-engineer | Feasibility and sequencing of each bet; platform boundary and contracts; reliability targets; security and governance |
| ml-engineer | AI capability strategy: model / agent / tool selection, evals, context and memory, quality–latency–cost trade-off over the year |
| ux-researcher | Segments and jobs to be done; for developer platforms, developer-experience pain points; how to validate cheaply in Q1 |
| product-designer | Experience strategy across surfaces; consistency vs per-surface needs |

Typical picks (up to 3): the roles whose lens matches the goals most directly.
