# Round type: build

A question about **building a product or feature**: what to build, how, and in what order.
Each role uses the lens in its own agent file, at the scale the goals describe. Phasing in
Recommendation: **MVP / later / not building**. Also answer this type's checklist:
[checklist.md](checklist.md).

Typical picks (up to 3): product-designer, ux-researcher, ml-engineer (when ranking, AI or
recommendations are involved), staff-software-engineer, data-scientist, competitive-analyst.
