# Overseer checklist

Answer each question for each report: **pass**, or **fail** with the exact fix (section, what to
change). A fix says what to write, not "improve X". Items 12–13 are also checked across reports.

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Does it open with a **TL;DR** that gives the answer, not the background? |
| 2 | Is there a **Terms** table, with every abbreviation and term of art defined? |
| 3 | Is every cross-reference to a project file, section or question a **relative link**? |
| 4 | Does every claim have a **source and a confidence level**? Are the fact-checker's "unverified" marks kept, not hidden? Were the numbers recomputed (see the fact-check notes)? |
| 5 | Are options compared on **advantages mapped to the success criteria (by ID), gaps, cost and maintenance**, with a stated reason for the pick? |
| 6 | Does every estimate **list its assumptions**? |
| 7 | Is every rate written as **numerator ÷ denominator**? |
| 8 | Is there a **happy-case** and a **complex-case** example? |
| 9 | Are **edge cases** listed? |
| 10 | Is **MVP vs later** separated, and does it say **what not to build**? |
| 11 | Is it **mobile-first**: nothing depends on hover? |
| 12 | Are **UI, data and engine consistent** with each other and with the other reports? |
| 13 | Is the **data shape** one that later features won't force a migration on? (Name the field or table at risk.) |
| 14 | Does every **open question** have a default and say what it blocks? |
